You'll hear 20 short music excerpts, 30 seconds each. In every trial you first get the original recording, followed by two processed versions of it. Listen to the original, then to both options, and pick the one you think does it best.

Please wear headphones, and set a comfortable volume before you start. The differences between the options can be subtle and are easy to miss on laptop or phone speakers.

All clips have been matched for loudness, so please leave your volume alone during the test. The order of the excerpts and of the two options is shuffled for every listener. The options may sound very alike. That's expected, so go with your honest impression.
